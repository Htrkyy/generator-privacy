This privacy policy applies to the Generator app for mobile devices (the “Application”), operated by Hüseyin Türk (the “Service Provider”).

Summary

  • Generator does not require an account and does not operate its own backend server.
  • Your favorites, history, settings, and unlocked generators are stored locally on your device.
  • All random results — including generated passwords and text you convert to Morse code — are created on your device and are never sent to the Service Provider.
  • The Application shows ads via Google AdMob, which may process device and advertising identifiers according to Google’s policies.
  • Questions about privacy: unecticdes@gmail.com.

Information Collection and Use

When you download and use the Application, information may include:

  • Device technical information such as Internet Protocol (IP) address and mobile operating system (typically processed by advertising / platform SDKs)
  • Advertising identifiers (for example Android Advertising ID), used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention
  • Local app data created by your use of the Application (favorite results, saved wheels and lists, generation history, generators unlocked by watching ads, language/theme preferences) — stored on your device only

Text you type into the Application (for example wheel or list options, or a sentence for the Morse code converter) and passwords produced by the password generator are processed locally and are not transmitted to the Service Provider. Content is only copied to your clipboard or shared with other apps when you explicitly tap “Copy” or “Share”.

The Service Provider does not ask you to create a user profile with name, email, or password inside the Application. If you contact the Service Provider by email, the Service Provider will receive the information you include in that message.
